# 🌐 San Pogodex — Pokémon GO Live Companion

**San Pogodex** is an all-in-one web companion built for Pokémon GO trainers. It provides real-time information on active in-game rotations, event calendars, battle calculations, and essential utility tools.

---

## ✨ Features & What the Platform Does

### ⚔️ Active Raids & Max Battles
- Track active **Mega**, **Super Mega**, **5-Star**, **3-Star**, **1-Star**, and **Shadow Raids**.
- Up-to-date **Dynamax** and **Gigantamax** battle bosses.
- Normal and weather-boosted CP ranges to quickly identify 100% IV catches.
- Detailed raid counters and battle difficulty recommendations.

### 🚀 Team GO Rocket Invasion Guide
- Current lineups for all Team GO Rocket members: **Grunts**, **Leaders** (Cliff, Sierra, Arlo), and **Giovanni**.
- Clearly shows which Pokémon are catchable after victory.
- Highlights Pokémon with available Shiny forms.

### 🥚 Egg Hatch Pools
- Up-to-date lists of Pokémon hatching from **2 km, 5 km, 7 km, 10 km, 11 km, and 12 km** eggs.
- Includes hatch tier rarities and shiny eligibility.

### 📜 Field & Special Research Tasks
- Searchable list of active field research tasks and their Pokémon encounter rewards.
- Shiny status markers for all reward Pokémon.

### 🌿 Wild Spawns & Biomes
- Current seasonal wild spawn pools and active nest rotations.

### 👥 Party Play Challenges
- Complete list of Party Play challenges and their encounter rewards.

### 🛡️ Type Effectiveness & Weakness Calculator
- Instant calculator for single and dual-type Pokémon with official Pokémon GO damage multipliers:
  - Double Weaknesses (2.56×)
  - Weaknesses (1.6×)
  - Resistances (0.625×)
  - Double Resistances / Immunities (0.391×)
  - Triple Resistances (0.244×)

### 🎁 Active Promo Codes
- Curated and verified active Pokémon GO promo codes ready for one-click redemption.
