# 🌐 SanDex — Pokémon GO Collection Tracker & Live Companion

**SanDex** is an all-in-one web platform and companion tool designed for Pokémon GO trainers. It provides a complete personal Pokédex collection tracker, a smart candy & evolution calculator, a trade-matcher system to coordinate with friends, priority to-do recommendations, and real-time active in-game rotation data.

---

## ✨ Features & What SanDex Does

### 📖 PokéDex Grid & Collection Tracker
- **Complete Regional Pokédex**: Track your collection across all generations and regions (Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar, Hisui, and Paldea).
- **Collection Statuses**: Mark individual Pokémon as *Caught*, *Missing*, or *Transferred Base*.
- **Live Progress & Stats**: Regional completion bars and overall Pokédex completion percentage.
- **Obtaining Method Filters**: Quickly filter Pokémon by how they are obtained — Evolutions, Wild Spawns, Egg Hatches, or Raids.
- **Smart Search & Sorting**: Search by name, Pokédex number, or typing. Sort ascending, descending, or alphabetically.
- **Bulk Actions**: "Catch All" and "Reset" buttons for filtered views.
- **Cloud Synchronization**: Create a free Trainer Account (via Email or Google Sign-In) to automatically save your collection in the cloud across all your devices.

---

### 🔍 Interactive Pokémon Details Modal
Tap any Pokémon card across the entire site to view comprehensive details:
- **How to Get**: Up-to-date guide detailing whether the Pokémon appears in the wild, eggs, raids, or requires evolution.
- **Evolution Flow**: Visual evolution chain showing candy costs, evolution items (Sinnoh Stone, Unova Stone, Metal Coat, etc.), and buddy walking requirements.
- **Base Stats**: Visual breakdown of Pokémon GO base Attack, Defense, and Stamina/HP.
- **Type Effectiveness**: Exact Pokémon GO combat multipliers (Double Weaknesses, Weaknesses, Resistances, and Immunities).

---

### 🍬 Candies & Evolving Calculator
- **Evolution Planning**: Enter your current candy count for any family to see exactly what you can evolve.
- **Buddy Distance Calculator**: Automatically computes the total walking distance (in kilometers) needed with your Buddy to evolve your missing Pokémon.
- **Organized Sections**:
  - **Ready to Evolve**: Species where you already have enough candies.
  - **Needs Candies**: Species requiring additional candy and buddy kilometers.
  - **Transferred / Missing Base**: Highlights when you need to re-catch the base Pokémon first.
- **Sortable**: Order families by candies needed or kilometers left to walk.

---

### 👥 Trainer Friends & Trade Matcher
- **Friend Connections**: Add fellow trainers by username with an integrated friend request notification system.
- **Side-by-Side Stats**: Compare Pokédex completion rates and regional totals with your friends.
- **Smart Trade Matcher**:
  - **"You can give them"**: Shows Pokémon you have caught that your friend is missing.
  - **"They can give you"**: Shows Pokémon your friend has that you are missing.
  - Effortlessly coordinate daily special trades and lucky trades to complete each other's Pokédex!

---

### 📋 Smart To-Do Guide
- **Personalized Checklist**: Automatically scans your Pokédex and compares it with active in-game events to generate an actionable plan:
  - **High Priority**: Missing or transferred Pokémon that are *currently available right now* in active wild spawns, raids, research quests, or eggs.
  - **Medium Priority**: Active encounters that award candies for evolution families where you are still short on candies.

---

### 🌀 Active In-Game Rotations
- **Events Calendar**: Active and upcoming Pokémon GO events with banners, descriptions, active bonuses, featured spawns, and real-time countdown timers.
- **Raid Bosses**: Super Mega, Mega, 5-Star, 3-Star, 1-Star, and Shadow Raids with CP ranges (normal & boosted), shiny eligibility, and direct Pokebattler counter links.
- **Max Battles**: Current Dynamax and Gigantamax bosses.
- **Egg Hatch Pools**: Complete hatch pool for 2 km, 5 km, 7 km, 10 km, 11 km, and 12 km eggs with rarity tiers and shiny indicators.
- **Field Research Quests**: Current PokéStop research tasks and encounter rewards.
- **Team GO Rocket Invasions**: Grunts, Team Leaders (Cliff, Sierra, Arlo), and Giovanni lineups with highlighted catchable slots and shiny forms.
- **Wild Spawns**: Active seasonal and biome spawn rotations.
- **Party Play**: Active Party challenges and encounter rewards.
- **Promo Codes**: Verified active Pokémon GO promo codes with one-click copy and direct links to the official Niantic Web Store.
